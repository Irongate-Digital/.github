# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

This organisation ships continuously from the default branch of each repository. Security
fixes are applied to the default branch; we do not back-port to older tags unless a release
is explicitly still in production.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions or
pull requests.**

Report privately to:

- **Email:** techsupport@irongatedigital.com
- **Subject prefix:** `[SECURITY] <repository> — <short summary>`

Please include as much of the following as you can:

- The repository and, if known, the affected file, endpoint or dependency.
- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- Any suggested remediation.
- Whether you would like to be credited.

## What to Expect

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement of your report | within 2 business days |
| Initial assessment and severity triage | within 5 business days |
| Fix or mitigation plan communicated | within 10 business days |

We will keep you informed of progress and will credit reporters in the release notes unless
you ask us not to.

## Scope

In scope:

- All repositories under the [Irongate-Digital](https://github.com/Irongate-Digital) organisation.
- Production services reachable at `*.irongatedigital.com` (where published).

Out of scope:

- Denial-of-service testing, spam, or social engineering.
- Findings from automated scanners submitted without a reproducible impact.
- Reports concerning third-party services we do not control.

Please act in good faith: give us reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure.
